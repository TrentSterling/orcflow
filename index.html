WHAT YOU ARE LOOKING AT

Around 95,000 orcs, every one of them steered, crowded, damaged and killed entirely on the GPU. One flow field bake per map edit, four WebGPU compute passes per frame, not a single orc on the CPU. It holds 60 fps with the compute pass under a millisecond, so the horde is not what costs you anything.

WHY IT EXISTS

Built in a day to answer one question: is the tech behind the big orc horde game a moat? It is not. Finishing a game still is. This one earns exactly zero dollars, which is the other half of the point.

About / More by Tront

ORCFLOW by Tront

ORCFLOW, subtitled Sir, We Have a Flow Field, is a free GPU tower defense game in Three.js. Every orc lives in a WebGPU storage buffer for its whole life: navigation over a flow field, crowd separation, damage, death and recycling all happen on the GPU, so the horde scales with your graphics card rather than your CPU.

One HTML file, no install, needs a desktop browser with WebGPU. Made by Tront (Trent Sterling) as one of a series of GPU crowd experiments that includes BIOMASS, BALLPIT and SEND DUDES.

More from Tront

  • BIOMASS: GPU horde tower defense with a colossus boss: tens of thousands of creatures in WebGPU compute.
  • SEND DUDES: Siege defense with 200,000 fighters simulated on the GPU. Hold the core, turn kills into reinforcements.
  • BALLPIT: GPU contact physics testbed: 20,000 XPBD particles in WebGPU compute with solid map collision.
