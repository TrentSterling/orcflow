WHAT YOU ARE LOOKING AT

Around 95,000 orcs, every one of them steered, crowded, damaged and killed entirely on the GPU. One flow field bake per map edit, four WebGPU compute passes per frame, not a single orc on the CPU. It holds 60 fps with the compute pass under a millisecond, so the horde is not what costs you anything.

WHY IT EXISTS

Built in a day to answer one question: is the tech behind the big orc horde game a moat? It is not. Finishing a game still is. This one earns exactly zero dollars, which is the other half of the point.
